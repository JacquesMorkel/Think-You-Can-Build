Think You Can Build is a live problem-solving show and graduate talent showcase.

Graduates take on the same business challenge in a facilitated session. You’ll have room to ask questions, test ideas, explain your choices, and show how you respond when things change.

It’s one more way to make your skills visible to the people who might be looking for them.
